# Week 1: Hold the Line (heavy week, 4 double days)
Clear 3 of 4 for the full reward, 2 of 4 for half, all 4 for a bonus. The tally checks these from the log.

- Sleep: 6h+ (G) on 4 of 7 days
- Fuel: Normal Fuel on 3 days, Minimum on the rest
- Move: 2 training sessions
- Learn: 4 Learn days, including at least 1 `+homelab` (a lab step or commit)

Reward: 100 XP, 25g (+40 XP for a full clear)
